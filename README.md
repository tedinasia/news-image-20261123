**"The World in One Day"** — one image that sums up Monday 23 November 2026.

A cracked globe sits at the centre of the composition: the planet itself
splitting apart over the COP31 climate summit, which collapsed in Antalya
after two weeks of talks without any agreement — the longest COP in history,
and the first to end with no overarching deal since Copenhagen in 2009. A
red **NO DEAL** stamp marks the moment, and the headline says it plainly:
*CLIMATE TALKS COLLAPSE*. The draft's fatal omission — the words "fossil
fuels" never made it into the final text — is spelled out underneath.

Six more stories from the same day orbit the lead in a 3×2 grid:

- **USA** — Democrats win the House with 258 seats, their biggest majority
  since 1992
- **GULF** — Iran hits Bahrain; Houthi drones strike Riyadh as the
  Iran–US war widens
- **BALTIC** — a Russian cargo ship burns off Finland after a drone strike
- **INDONESIA** — 3 dead, dozens missing in a candy-plant factory fire
- **SEOUL** — the Education Minister quits over an AI exam-cheating scandal
- **SPORT** — India post a record 720/9d Test total in Wellington

## How it runs

`main.py` is standard-library only: it copies `entry.png` (1024×1024,
under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`) and
validates the PNG signature, dimensions, size, and a byte-identical copy
via SHA-256.
